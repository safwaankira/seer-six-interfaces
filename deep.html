Information finance protocol

Turn any question into a price you can act on.

Seer is an information finance protocol. Markets turn what people and AI agents know into prices that funders, DAOs, reviewers and risk teams can use before they decide. Outcomes are ERC-20 tokens, answers come from Reality.eth, and disputes go to Kleros Court. Five apps sit on top, each built for a different kind of decision.

Lifecycle

How every Seer market resolves

1

Ask and trade

Anyone opens a market from a Reality.eth question. Collateral splits into ERC-20 outcome tokens that trade on any DEX, and merge back into collateral at any time.

2

Answer with a bond

When the answer window opens, someone posts the answer to Reality.eth with a bond. Anyone who disagrees posts double the bond.

3

Dispute in Kleros Court

Contested answers go to Kleros jurors. Once final, winning tokens redeem for collateral. An Invalid answer splits it back.
